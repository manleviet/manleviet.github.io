Hi👋! I am a Project Assistant and PhD Candidate at the [Institute of Software Engineering and Artificial Intelligence](https://www.tugraz.at/institute/ist/home){:target="_blank"} of [Graz University of Technology](https://www.tugraz.at/){:target="_blank"} (TU Graz), Austria, working on Knowledge-based Diagnosis, Configuration Systems, Software Product Lines, and Explanations in AI.

Under the supervision of [Professor Alexander Felfernig](https://felfernig.sai.tugraz.at){:target="_blank"}, my doctoral research — *Intelligent Techniques for Efficient Diagnostic Reasoning in Constraint-Based Systems* — develops solver-agnostic algorithms for conflict detection, diagnosis, and quality assurance that scale to industrial constraint-based systems, including configuration systems and recommender systems. More recent work extends these foundations toward causality-based explanations and Large Language Models for Software Product Line Engineering.

Before joining TU Graz in 2020, I spent more than 15 years at the [University of Economics, Hue University](https://hce.edu.vn/){:target="_blank"}, Vietnam, as Lecturer and Vice-Dean (2014–2019) of the Faculty of Economic Information Systems, teaching Programming, and Data Structures and Algorithms, while researching Recommender Systems and Information Visualization, particularly *multi-agent models for visualizing relationships in collaborative filtering*. I obtained my Master of Computer Science (2008–2011) at the [Institute for Informatics in French-speaking areas](https://ifi.vnu.edu.vn/en/about/){:target="_blank"}, Vietnam, with double diplomas from the [University of La Rochelle](https://www.univ-larochelle.fr/){:target="_blank"}, France, and [Hanoi University of Science and Technology](https://hust.edu.vn/){:target="_blank"}, Vietnam. During my Master's study, I worked on Machine Learning, Content-based Information Retrieval, and Information Visualization, and completed my Master's thesis — an image browser based on Self-Organizing Maps — as part of an internship at the [Catholic University of Louvain](https://uclouvain.be/){:target="_blank"}, Belgium, under the supervision of [Professor Michel Verleysen](https://www.uclouvain.be/en/people/michel.verleysen){:target="_blank"}.

I am now looking forward to research positions in academia and industry where I can continue working on Explainability, Knowledge-based Reasoning, Constraint Acquisition, and the integration of symbolic and learning-based methods.
